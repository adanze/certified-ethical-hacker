      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is the definition of information security?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>The state of well-being of information and infrastructure in which the possibility of theft, tampering, and disruption of information and services is low or tolerable.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Information Security Overview</a>
      </div>
    </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What are the five elements of information security?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Authenticity, Non-Repudiation (CIA + AN).</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is Confidentiality and what controls enforce it?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Assurance that information is accessible only to authorized users. Controls: data classification, encryption, secure disposal of equipment.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is Integrity and what controls enforce it?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Trustworthiness of data; prevention of improper or unauthorized changes. Controls: checksums, access control.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is Availability and what controls enforce it?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Systems are accessible when required by authorized users. Controls: redundant disk arrays, clustered machines, antivirus, DDoS prevention systems.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is Authenticity and what controls enforce it?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Ensures data and communications are genuine and uncorrupted. Controls: biometrics, smart cards, digital certificates.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flashcard">
    <div class="flashcard-inner">
      <div class="flashcard-front">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Question</span>
        <p>What is Non-Repudiation and how is it enforced?</p>
      </div>
      <div class="flashcard-back">
        <span class="flashcard-label">Answer</span>
        <p>Guarantee that the sender cannot deny sending a message and the recipient cannot deny receiving it. Enforced via digital signatures.</p>
        <a class="flashcard-link" href="../information-security-overview/#elements-of-information-security">&#x2192; Elements of Information Security</a>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
